Privacy Policy

TalkBoard AAC by Ayra Talk · Last updated 2 November 2026

TalkBoard is a communication board that runs in your web browser. We built it so your data stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and the app doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The app saves these in your browser's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) so it works offline and remembers your setup:

  • Your boards, tiles, phrases and settings
  • Your lock PIN
  • Voice recordings or audio files you upload
  • Board files you import

This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Text-to-speech

Speech uses your device's built-in voices. Some browsers offer online voices run by the browser maker (for example Google or Microsoft). If you pick one of those, the text being spoken is handled under that company's privacy policy. Pick a voice installed on your device to keep everything offline.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the domain uses Cloudflare for DNS. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical details such as your IP address when the page loads. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

TalkBoard can be used by children with help from a parent, carer or therapist. Since we collect no personal information, we hold none about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
