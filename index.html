🔏 Privacy Notice

What data is stored?

  • Communication boards and custom tiles you create.
  • Usage trends (tile tap counts and timestamps).
  • Settings preferences (theme, volume, speed, mode).
  • Uploaded voice samples (stored as Base64 in localStorage).

Where is it stored?

All data is stored exclusively on your device using browser localStorage. No data is ever sent to any server, analytics service, or third party.

How to delete?

Use Restore Defaults above to erase all stored data. You can also clear localStorage via your browser's site settings.

Device sharing notice

Data is visible to anyone with access to this device and browser. Lock the app when not in use to prevent unintended changes.
